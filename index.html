Xtream Codes login in Smarters Pro: in 5 steps (2026)

✍️ Samuel Hughes · 📅 October 2026 · ⏱️ 3 min read · 🛡️ Verified Review

To log into Smarters Pro using Xtream Codes, open the app, choose “Login with Xtream Codes,” enter your DNS, username, and password from your provider, then tap “Login.” The whole process takes about 30 seconds.

You do not need a free trial — only a paid subscription with active credentials.

What you need

  • A supported device (e.g., Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Android TV Box, Apple TV) with Smarters Pro installed.
  • Your Xtream Codes login details — these are provided by your IPTV subscription service. For example, premium services like SubTV IP (subtvip.store) give you a DNS URL, username, and password after purchase.
  • An internet connection — Wi‑Fi or Ethernet. For best performance, set your DNS to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) to reduce ISP throttling.
  1. Open Smarters Pro on your device. You will see the welcome screen with three login options.
  2. Select “Login with Xtream Codes” – it is the middle button on the home screen.
  3. Enter your DNS URL in the first field. This is the server address your provider gave you (e.g., http://your-provider.com:8080).
  4. Type your username and password in the next two fields. These are case-sensitive — double‑check for typos.
  5. Tap “Login” and wait a few seconds. The app will validate your credentials and load your channel list and EPG. You are now ready to stream.

If a step fails

Make sure you entered the DNS exactly as provided (including “http://” or “https://” and the port number). If the app says “Invalid credentials,” contact your provider via their support channel (e.g., WhatsApp for SubTV IP). Also check that your device date and time are correct — an incorrect clock can block authentication. For buffering issues, set the buffer/cache to 5,000 ms in the app’s player settings (navigate to “Settings > Player > Buffer size”).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Xtream Codes in Smarters Pro?

Xtream Codes is a login method used by many IPTV providers. It requires a DNS URL, username, and password. Smarters Pro supports this method directly, so you do not need to load an M3U playlist file.

Where do I get the Xtream Codes login details?

You receive them from your IPTV subscription after payment. For example, SubTV IP (subtvip.store) emails your DNS, username, and password right after your order is activated — there is no waiting period.

Can I use the same login on multiple devices?

It depends on your subscription plan. Many premium services allow connections on one or two IP addresses simultaneously. Check the terms of your plan. Smarters Pro can be installed on Fire TV, Android TV, Apple TV, and smart TVs without extra cost.

What if the app says “Login failed” or “No response from server”?

First, restart your device and router. Then verify that the DNS URL is typed correctly. If the problem persists, your provider may be performing maintenance or your subscription may have expired. Contact support via WhatsApp — SubTV IP support is available 24/7.

Do I need to change DNS settings on my device?

Not required, but changing your device’s DNS to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) can help reduce ISP throttling and improve stream stability. On Fire TV, go to “Settings > Network > Your Wi‑Fi > Advanced > IP settings > Static > DNS 1: 1.1.1.1”.

Is there an instant activation for Smarters Pro or for Xtream Codes login?

No. Smarters Pro is a free app, but the Xtream Codes credentials come from a paid subscription. There is no free trial, test period, or “24‑hour” access — you must purchase a plan (1, 3, or 12 months) to use the service.

How do I update my playlist after logging in?

Smarters Pro automatically refreshes the channel list and EPG each time you open the app. If you need a manual refresh, go to “Settings > EPG > Force update” or simply close and reopen the app.
